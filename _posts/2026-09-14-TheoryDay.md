The UMass Amherst Theory Group is continuing to organize 'New England Theory Day' this year, with the goal of bringing together theoretical computer scientists from around the region. It will be an all-day event on October 02 2026, held on UMass's campus. The day will feature talks by invited speakers, poster and lightening talk sessions for graduate students, and social events.

For more details, see: https://theory.cs.umass.edu/theory-day
The registration form: https://forms.gle/N4bcW5Q5JYyKYgbV9

Registration is free, but we are asking everyone to register by Thursday, September 24 2026.
